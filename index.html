A few useful details

Before we begin.

What does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, the features you need, and how much content is ready. Tell us a little about your project and we’ll put together a clear scope and quote before work begins.

Can you update my existing website?

Yes. Send us your current website and what you’d like to improve. We’ll look at its design and structure, then discuss whether a focused refresh or a new build makes the most sense.

What should I have ready?

A short description of your business, your goals for the site, and any existing branding, photos, or text are a helpful start. It’s fine if you don’t have everything figured out—we can work through the direction together.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the scope and how quickly content and feedback are available. We’ll agree on a timeline at the start, so you know what to expect at each stage.
